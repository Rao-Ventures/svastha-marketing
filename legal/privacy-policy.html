Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Scope and Information We Collect

This notice explains how Rao Ventures LLC handles personal information in the US version of Svastha, including its mobile app, App Clip, and web app.

We collect:

  • Account information: name, email, sign-in details, and preferences. Apple or Google may provide profile information when you choose their sign-in service. Apple may provide a private relay email. Our authentication provider handles passwords securely.
  • App content and activity: reflections, bookmarks, reading history, progress, and reminder preferences.
  • AI information: questions and recent chat context you choose to send, your AI-sharing permission, and reports you submit.
  • Purchase information: account and transaction identifiers, purchase dates, plan, and subscription status. Apple or Google processes payments; we do not receive payment card details.
  • Device and usage information: device and session identifiers, operating system, app version, IP address, approximate location, screens visited, interactions, and diagnostic information. Analytics run automatically and some events are linked to your account.
  • Support information: messages and information you provide when contacting us.

Your reflections, questions, and activity may reveal religious beliefs or health-related information. Providing private content and using AI are optional.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and synchronize app features, manage accounts and subscriptions, respond to support and reports, send requested reminders and service notices, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We use sensitive content to provide requested features, review submitted reports, protect security, and meet legal obligations. We do not use private reflections or AI conversations for advertising or to train our own general-purpose AI models.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use it for targeted advertising, or profile users for decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Optional AI and Reports

Before using AI, you are asked to permit sharing your question with OpenAI for scripture search, and your question, up to six previous chat messages, and selected scripture passages with Google Gemini for an answer. These providers receive the submitted text. Their retention and use are governed by the applicable service terms and agreements.

You can decline or withdraw permission in Chat. Withdrawal stops future requests but does not recall information already processed. AI answers may be inaccurate and are not professional advice.

To report an answer, tap Report answer, review and edit the included text, and select a reason to send it. We receive that text, the reason, your account ID, and submission time for review. Other chat messages are not attached.

5. Security and Retention

We use safeguards such as secure transmission and access controls to protect personal information. No system is completely secure.

  • Accounts and saved content: retained while needed to provide the service, until you delete the content or account, subject to lawful retention exceptions.
  • AI chats: the app does not save a persistent conversation history. Providers process submitted messages as described above.
  • AI reports: removed after 30 days through daily cleanup, or when you delete your account.
  • AI permission: retained until withdrawn or the account is deleted.
  • Billing, support, and diagnostic records: retained as needed for their stated purposes, including accounting, security, dispute handling, and legal requirements.

Deletion removes account content from our active systems. Backups and provider records may follow separate deletion schedules; limited records may be retained for legitimate legal, accounting, or security needs. Local copies on other devices are not immediately erased. Contact us about retention or deletion relevant to your data.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can change reminders and preferences in Profile, withdraw AI permission in Chat, and delete your account through Profile > Delete Account. If you cannot use the app, email support@raoventures.co with Delete my Svastha account in the subject. We may verify your identity; Apple-linked accounts may require reauthentication. You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion.

Account deletion and uninstalling do not cancel subscriptions. Cancel separately through the Apple or Google account used to purchase. Cancellation is not required before requesting deletion.

Depending on applicable state law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of information; limit certain sensitive-information uses; opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or certain profiling; and appeal a denied request. We do not discriminate unlawfully for exercising these rights. Authorized agents may submit requests where permitted, subject to verification.

Submit requests by email to support@raoventures.co, by phone at +1 (737) 434-0575, or by mail below. Provide your account email where relevant, but do not send passwords or private reflections. We respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain any permitted extension or denial.

Our Cookie and App Storage Policy explains browser and device storage. Browser controls may limit storage but may not stop app analytics. Contact us about privacy requests.

7. Children's Privacy

Svastha is intended for adults at least 18 who have reached the age of majority. It is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as required.

8. Changes

We will update this notice when practices change and provide legally required notice. Where consent is required, we will obtain it before the relevant processing; continued use alone does not replace consent.
