Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Company: Rao Ventures LLC

Privacy Contact: support@raoventures.co

1. Information and Use

This policy covers Svastha's US mobile app and App Clip.

We process account and contact details; reflections, bookmarks, reading progress, and reminder preferences; optional AI requests, permissions, and reports; purchase and subscription records; and support messages. Apple and Google process payments; we do not receive payment card details.

We also process device and session identifiers, operating system, app version, IP address, approximate location, interactions, and diagnostics. Installation and preview timing are stored locally to manage preview access and reminders.

We use this information to provide features, manage accounts and subscriptions, deliver requested reminders, respond to support, improve reliability, protect security, and meet legal obligations.

Your content may reveal religious beliefs or health information. Providing this content and using AI are optional. We use sensitive information for requested features, submitted reports, security, and legal obligations, obtaining consent where required. We do not use private reflections or AI conversations for advertising or to train our own general-purpose AI models.

2. Sharing and Optional AI

Supabase processes accounts, saved content, and app requests. Apple and Google handle optional sign-in; they and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information. Expo processes connection information for hosting and distribution.

Vexo Analytics automatically receives device, session, usage, preview-notice, purchase-screen, and diagnostic information in production on supported physical devices. Our custom events exclude account identifiers and private content. Browser cookie controls may not stop app analytics.

With your permission, OpenAI receives your question for scripture search. Google Gemini receives your question, up to six previous chat messages, and selected scripture passages to generate an answer. You can decline permission in Chat or withdraw it in Profile > Settings > Withdraw AI permission; withdrawal stops future requests but does not recall information already processed.

Reporting an AI answer sends the text you review, your selected reason, account ID, and submission time to us. Other chat messages are not attached.

Providers may process information in the United States or other countries under their applicable terms and agreements. We may also disclose information to meet legal obligations, protect rights and security, or complete a business transfer with required protections and notice.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use targeted advertising, or conduct profiling with legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Retention and Security

We retain accounts and saved content until deletion or while needed to provide the service, subject to lawful retention exceptions. AI reports are removed after 30 days or account deletion. AI permission records remain until withdrawal or account deletion. The app does not store persistent AI chat history; providers' retention follows their applicable terms.

Local preview information may remain after sign-out or account deletion until app data is cleared; backups may restore it. Limited billing, support, diagnostic, and backup records may remain as needed for accounting, security, disputes, or legal obligations. Local copies on other devices are not immediately erased.

We use secure transmission and access controls. No system is completely secure.

4. Account Deletion

Open Profile > Delete Account. After preview expiry, use Account & settings to reach Profile. No subscription purchase or cancellation is required first.

Without the app, email support@raoventures.co with the subject Delete my Svastha account, preferably from your account email. No reinstall or new account is required. We verify identity; Apple-linked accounts may require reauthentication. Contact us if you need another verification method.

Deletion permanently removes your account, reflections, bookmarks, reading progress, streaks, AI permissions, and AI reports from active systems. In-app deletion also cancels reminders and clears their preferences on that device. Limited records, backups, provider records, and local preview timing may remain as described in Section 3. We respond within applicable legal deadlines.

Account deletion and uninstalling do not cancel app-store subscriptions. Cancel separately through the Apple or Google account used to purchase to stop renewal charges.

5. Your Choices and Rights

Manage reminders in Profile and withdraw AI permission in Profile > Settings > Withdraw AI permission.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a portable copy of your information; limit sensitive-information use; opt out of sale, targeted advertising, sharing, or certain profiling; and appeal a denied request. We do not discriminate unlawfully for exercising these rights. Authorized agents may act where permitted, subject to verification.

Submit requests or appeals using the contact details below. We may verify identity and respond within applicable legal deadlines, explaining extensions, denials, and available appeal or regulatory complaint options. Do not send passwords or private reflections.

6. Children and Updates

Svastha is for adults aged 18 or older who have reached the age of majority. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information.

We update this policy when practices change and provide notice or obtain consent where legally required.
